# Audit C - Live Pulse CRM on small screens (https://pulse-crm-weld.vercel.app)
Read-only pass. Viewports: mobile 375x812 (all listed pages) and tablet 768x1024 (subset). Nothing was created, edited, saved or submitted.
Method: per page, `document.documentElement.scrollWidth > innerWidth`, `main.scrollWidth > main.clientWidth`, a scan of elements past the viewport edge that are outside a scroll container, a tap-target scan (<28px), and a screenshot.

Format: SEVERITY | viewport | page | what is wrong | how to reproduce | suspected cause/file

## Checks that passed
- Horizontal page scroll: `documentElement.scrollWidth > innerWidth` was false on every page audited, at 375 and 768. `main` overflow was false everywhere. The one "main" scrollWidth > clientWidth case I saw was a wrong-viewport reading and was re-run correctly.
- Mobile menu: the header hamburger ("Open menu", 32x32) opens the sidebar drawer (all 22 links at 36px height). The X closes it. On /dashboard/settings the drawer shows the settings nav (Back to app, Account: Profile/Security/Preferences/Notifications, Workspace: Integrations/Email Accounts/WhatsApp/LinkedIn/AI Assistant/Automation/Lead Finder/Billing).
- Header action rows wrap correctly (leads: AI Score All / Recalculate All / Import / Export / Add Lead).
- Tab strips scroll horizontally (overview, contacts, analytics, campaigns, sequences, lead-finder, settings) and are not clipped without scrolling.
- Tables scroll inside their own containers (none escape to page scroll).
- Copilot stacks vertically on phones. It is side-by-side at 768 and fine.
- Inbox: usable on phone (conversation list, search, tabs, Compose). The test data is empty, so the list-to-thread navigation on phones could not be verified. At 768 it is a 2-pane layout and is fine.
- Lead detail and customer detail pages: no overflow. Lead detail header fits (Edit Lead plus More).
- Command palette fits the phone viewport.

## Process notes (for the caller)
- Browser tabs are shared among parallel audit agents. A batch action without an explicit tabId hit another agent's tab (tab-15). I briefly drove it: a resize to mobile and navigation to /dashboard/lead-finder/overview. I restored its URL to /dashboard/settings?tab=ai but left its viewport at the mobile size. Another agent also navigated my tab-17. After tab-17 disappeared I continued in tab-19 and reset it to desktop.
- Not tested: the inbox list-to-thread flow (no data), the copilot chat workspace (would trigger AI), Filters popovers, and modals that create data (Add Lead, Compose, etc.).
